# Support

Published by AXSync, www.axsync.com.

## How to get help

1. Email hunain@axsync.com, or use the Contact the Author option on the Odoo Apps Store page of this product.
2. Include: Odoo version, module version, Odoo.sh or self-hosted, the Shopify API version set on the store, what you expected, what happened, and the matching lines of the Shopify Logs or Queue (Shopify > Monitoring).

## What is covered

Defects in the delivered module and questions about how it behaves. Custom workflows, extra fields, a migration of historical orders, and help with your Shopify app setup or your reverse proxy are separate work.

## Reporting a defect

Describe the steps to reproduce. For a connection problem, send the text of the error on the store form. For an order that did not import, send its Shopify number and the Queue entry.
